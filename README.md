## Selected execution evidence

These are concrete engineering results, not just architecture diagrams. Each link opens the original evidence, including what ran, what failed, what was recovered, and what remains outside the demonstrated scope.

- **[Salesforce and Snowflake: recover an uncertain CRM write](https://github.com/rlbsem/customer-signal-activation/blob/main/docs/evidence/connected-integration/README.md).** What if Salesforce saves an approved action but the response is lost? In a recorded connected Salesforce Developer Edition and Snowflake run, the dispatcher recovered the existing receipt rather than creating a duplicate. An independent reader verified the record, and CRM feedback changed the warehouse recommendation from `clarify_need` to `hold`. This demonstrates controlled CRM execution and warehouse feedback, not customer outreach or a sale.

- **[BigQuery: distinguish GA4 purchases from item rows](https://github.com/rlbsem/first-party-measurement-assurance/blob/main/docs/evidence/google-bigquery/README.md).** Native GoogleSQL ran against Google's public, obfuscated GA4 ecommerce sample. The queries found **14 purchase events, 57 nested item rows, 13 distinct transaction IDs, and two unresolved item/revenue differences**. This exposes the reporting error caused by counting unnested items as purchases. The native sample is separate from the synthetic contract and payment reconciliation; it is not proof of live GA4 ingestion.

- **[PostgreSQL CDC: migrate without silently corrupting customer state](https://github.com/rlbsem/martech-migration-assurance/blob/main/docs/evidence/postgres-cdc/report.md).** Native `pgoutput` capture bridges a consistent snapshot to subsequent WAL changes. Thirteen executed fault scenarios cover cutover, process termination, lost acknowledgments and unsafe checkpoint or schema states. An equal-row-count migration still fails when an unknown subscription value becomes false. Unsupported recovery paths stay blocked instead of being declared successful.

- **[Qwen3 model release: evaluate, promote, and roll back](https://github.com/rlbsem/enterprise-agent-runtime-evaluation/blob/main/docs/live-release-acceptance.md).** Actual local Qwen3-14B inference tested whether an account-risk agent could make evidence-supported decisions and safe authorized actions. An earlier candidate was rejected at **35/36**; a later bounded-reasoning release passed **48/48 synthetic tasks per configuration** and completed local shadow, read-only canary, promotion and fault-triggered rollback. The results are finite test evidence, not production certification.

- **[Financial reporting: respect acquisition boundaries and revisions](https://github.com/rlbsem/enterprise-acquisition-onboarding/blob/main/docs/annual-scenario.md).** A runnable synthetic three-company scenario demonstrates why identical local IDs, pre-acquisition records and held businesses cannot automatically enter consolidated reporting. PostgreSQL/dbt publications preserve original and restated values. The annual report shows **$20.01M to $20.06M in consolidated net invoicing** and retains separate **$25K posting** and **$40K unsupported intercompany** findings. These are synthetic accounting inputs, not recognized revenue or client results.

- **[SQL/dbt performance: measure correctness before claiming an optimization](https://github.com/rlbsem/revenue-metrics-platform/blob/main/docs/performance.md).** The Revenue Metrics Platform models bookings, invoices, cash and ARR at their own grains across **15 SQL/dbt models**. Controlled local enterprise and stress benchmarks compare complete output rowsets with an independent full build. A proposed selective rebuild was rejected after end-to-end measurement showed it was slower, despite reducing some work. Native Snowflake fixture acceptance is documented separately from the large local benchmarks.

- **[Control-plane workload: isolate failing destinations and expose rate limits](https://github.com/rlbsem/enterprise-martech-ai-control-plane/blob/main/docs/evidence/dependency-isolation/arrival-review/README.md).** PostgreSQL-governed permits, bounded queues and durable receiver receipts were tested under substantial synthetic load. Across four recorded runs, **12,035 admitted actions** reconciled to single effects while **7,965 overload rejections** were accounted for. Independent receiver analysis also found some one-second arrival buckets with **11 requests** despite a ten-per-window permit contract. The system records that boundary rather than falsely claiming strict downstream arrival-rate compliance.
